About DailyForge

DailyForge is a free collection of practical utility tools built for real everyday use. No accounts, no tracking, no advertisements — just fast, clean tools that work offline.

Why DailyForge exists

Most online tool websites are either overloaded with rarely-used gadgets or filled with intrusive ads. We focused on high-frequency tasks people actually do every day: converting units while shopping, splitting a restaurant bill, checking BMI, calculating EMI, generating a strong password, timing a focus session, or converting text case.

What you get

  • 14 focused tools with zero clutter
  • Works completely offline after first visit (Progressive Web App)
  • Installable on phone and desktop like a native app
  • All calculations run locally in your browser — nothing is sent to a server
  • Mobile-first design with large touch targets
  • No sign-up, no cookies for tracking, no analytics

Tools included

  • Unit Converter (length, weight, temperature, volume)
  • Tip & Bill Splitter
  • Percentage Calculator
  • Discount Calculator
  • Age Calculator
  • BMI Calculator
  • EMI / Loan Calculator
  • Password Generator
  • Pomodoro Timer
  • Stopwatch
  • Word Counter
  • Case Converter
  • Color Converter (HEX ↔ RGB)
  • Date Difference Calculator

Privacy first

Everything runs on your device. We do not collect personal data, do not use analytics, and do not show ads. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
