Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

DailyForge is designed to respect your privacy completely.

What we collect

We collect nothing.

  • No personal data
  • No analytics or tracking scripts
  • No advertising cookies
  • No accounts or logins
  • No server-side storage of your inputs

Local processing

All calculations (unit conversion, tip splitting, BMI, EMI, password generation, timers, etc.) happen entirely inside your browser. Your numbers, text, and generated passwords never leave your device.

Offline & caching

As a Progressive Web App, DailyForge may cache its own files (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, icons) on your device so the tools work offline. This cache lives only on your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

Third-party resources

We load Three.js (for the 3D background) and Google Fonts from public CDNs. These services may receive standard technical requests such as IP address and user-agent. No personal content or calculation data is sent to them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.

Contact

Because we store no data about you, there is nothing to request or delete. You can simply stop using the app at any time.
