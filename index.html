About DailyForge

DailyForge is a collection of practical, everyday tools designed for real daily use. No accounts, no tracking, no ads.

Most “tool” websites are either packed with rarely-used gadgets or buried under advertisements. We focused on high-frequency tasks: converting units while shopping, splitting a restaurant bill, checking BMI, generating a strong password, timing focus sessions, calculating EMI, and more.

Why these tools?

  • High real-world daily need
  • Relatively low competition / less saturated
  • Fast and work completely offline
  • Clean interface that respects your attention
